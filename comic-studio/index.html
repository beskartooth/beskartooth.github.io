Tools

V Select

C Cut

B Speech bubble

T Thought bubble

K Caption

S Sound effect

Shift Snap a cut horizontal or vertical

Image

Scroll Zoom the selected picture while the pointer is over it

Ctrl+Scroll Zoom that picture toward the pointer

Alt+Scroll Rotate 1° per notch

Ctrl+V Paste a clipboard image into the selected panel, or into the bank, when that image is newer than a copied element

Shift+Alt+Scroll Rotate in 15° steps

Shift while dragging the handle snaps to 15°

Side panel: flip horizontal, flip vertical, turn 90°, reset rotation, reset framing

View

Ctrl+0 Fit the page in the window

Ctrl+1 100%, actual pixels

+ − Zoom the view in or out

Ctrl+Scroll Zoom the view toward the pointer, unless the pointer is over the selected picture or lettering

Corner menu: Fit, 50%, 100%, 200%, 400%

Space+drag Pan

Middle-mouse drag Pan

Scroll Scroll the page, except over the selected picture

Lettering

Drag to move. Double-click to edit. Drag handles to resize, rotate, or move a tail

Ctrl+Scroll Resize the selected lettering

Alt+Scroll Rotate it 1° (Shift snaps to 15°)

Delete Remove it

Editing

Ctrl+C Copy the selected lettering or picture

Ctrl+X Cut the selection

Ctrl+V Paste it, offset a little more each time

Ctrl+D Duplicate the selection

Ctrl+Z Undo

Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z Redo

Delete or Backspace Delete the selection

Ctrl+S Save the project

Esc Close this panel, cancel a cut, or return to Select
